The Crisis

A Growing Environmental Emergency

India generates over 113,000 tonnes of menstrual waste annually. Most of it ends up in landfills, water bodies, or is burned in the open — polluting our environment and endangering public health.

Landfill Overload

Sanitary pads are non-biodegradable, taking 500–800 years to decompose. They leach microplastics and toxic chemicals into soil and groundwater, creating a ticking environmental time bomb.

0 Tonnes of menstrual waste per year in India

Water Contamination

Improper disposal into water bodies releases dioxins, furans, and microplastics — poisoning aquatic ecosystems and contaminating drinking water sources.

Open Burning Hazards

Burning pads releases toxic dioxins and furans — known carcinogens that cause respiratory diseases, hormonal disruption, and severe air pollution in communities.

Health Risks for Workers

Waste workers who handle unsegregated menstrual waste face exposure to bloodborne pathogens, infections, and biohazard materials without proper protection.

The Process

How Clean-X Pad Works

A six-stage automated process that transforms hazardous menstrual waste into safe, manageable material — all within minutes.

01

Smart Intake

An automated intake slot accepts the used sanitary pad. IR sensors detect insertion and initiate the processing cycle automatically.

02

UV Sterilization

High-intensity UV-C light (254nm wavelength) irradiates the pad for 60 seconds, destroying 99.9% of bacteria, viruses, and bloodborne pathogens.

03

Controlled Heating

Precision-controlled heating elements raise the temperature to 150–200°C, drying and reducing the pad volume by up to 80% while containing all emissions.

04

Shredding

Industrial-grade micro-shredding blades break down the dried pad into fine, unrecognizable fragments — ensuring complete destruction and volume reduction.

05

Activated Carbon Filtration

Multi-layer activated carbon filters capture all odors, volatile organic compounds, and micro-pollutants — ensuring zero emissions and zero odor.

06

Safe Waste Collection

Processed material is collected in a sealed, replaceable container. The sterile residue is safe for standard waste disposal — reducing biohazard risk to zero.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clean-X Pad is a smart, automated sanitary waste management device that safely disposes of used sanitary pads through a six-stage process: Smart Intake, UV Sterilization, Controlled Heating, Shredding, Activated Carbon Filtration, and Safe Waste Collection. It's designed to be a sustainable, zero-emission alternative to open burning and landfill disposal.

Unlike traditional incinerators that release toxic dioxins and furans into the atmosphere, Clean-X Pad uses controlled heating (not incineration) combined with activated carbon filtration to ensure zero emissions. It also adds UV sterilization for pathogen elimination and is fully automated — requiring no manual operation.

Absolutely. Clean-X Pad is designed with multiple safety features including a fully enclosed, tamper-proof body, auto-shutoff mechanisms, temperature monitoring, and safe sealed waste collection. It produces no flames, no smoke, and no harmful emissions — making it safe for schools, hospitals, offices, and any public space.

Clean-X Pad is engineered for energy efficiency, consuming minimal electricity per processing cycle. Its low power requirement makes it compatible with solar panel setups, enabling deployment in off-grid and rural areas where conventional power supply may be unreliable.

After processing, the waste is reduced by up to 80% in volume and is completely sterilized. The resulting dry, inert material is collected in a sealed container and can be safely disposed of with standard municipal waste — posing no biohazard or environmental risk.
